## How to read Quality vs Final

Lists are ranked by **quality_score** (then final_score). Final = quality × evidence_factor + org_boost. Final below Quality with org boost 0 means the evidence_factor discounted the rubric; that is correct.

**Org standing** is a property of the organisation (constant across papers). **Org boost** is how much of that standing this paper's verified affiliation evidence earns. Org name is shown as a label, not a sort key.
